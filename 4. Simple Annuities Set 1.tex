\Heading
{Financial Literacy}
{Simple Annuities - Set 1}
{\textbf{Instructions: }For each problem, sketch a timeline, write an equation
  in one variable, and solve for that variable to answer the question.}

\begin{questions}

  \mathproblem[][1.75in]{
    Find the accumulated value of a simple annuity of \$2,000 per year for 5 years
    if money is worth 9\% per year.
  }

  \mathproblem[][1.75in]{
    It is estimated that a machine will need to be replaced 10 years from now at a
    cost of \$80,000. How much must be set aside each year to provide that amount, if
    you can earn 8\% interest per year?
  }

  \mathproblem[][2in]{
    Jeff buys a used car by paying \$3000 down and \$300 per month for 3 years.  What was the
    equivalent up-front cash price of the car, if the interest rate on the loan is 9\% compounded monthly?
  }

  \mathproblem[][1.75in]{
    To settle a debt with interest at 12\% compounded semiannually, Leonard agrees to make 15
    payments of \$400 at the end of each half-year, and then a final payment, 6 months later, of \$292.39.
    What was the original amount of the debt?
  }

  \mathproblem[][1.75in]{
    A company is trying to decide whether to buy or lease some equipment.

    \bpoint They can buy it for \$40,000, and then incur maintenance costs of \$400 per month, payable
    at the end of each month.  At the end of 6 years, they expect to be able to sell the
    used equipment for \$5,000.

    \bpoint Alternatively, they could lease the equipment \$1,200 per month, payable at the end
    of each month, for 6 years, and the lessor would pay maintenance costs.  After 6 years, the
    lessor would take back the equipment.

    If the company can earn 18\% compounded monthly on capital, would it make more sense to
    buy or lease?
  }

\end{questions}
