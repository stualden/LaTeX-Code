{\textbf{Instructions: }For each problem, sketch a timeline, write an equation
        in one variable, and solve for that variable to answer the question.}

\begin{questions}
        \Problem{Five (5) years ago, you put \$100 in a savings account earning 3.5\%
                interest.  How much do you have in the account now?}

        \Problem{You just lent a friend \$300 and told him you'd like him to repay you
                someday at 7\% annual interest.  How much does he owe you if he pays
                you back two years from now?}

        \Problem{Same situation as in problem 2, but your friend pays you back in only
                6 months.  How much does he owe you then?}

        \Problem{You buy shares in a mutual fund for \$150.  After 6 years, you sell
                your shares for \$212.78. What annual rate of interest did you earn?}

        \Problem{You buy \$1,000 worth of stock in a company whose stock price is
                growing at a rate of 10\% per year.  How many years must you wait
                before the stock is worth \$1,250?}

        \Problem{At 5\% annual interest, how long does it take for your money to triple?}

\end{questions}
